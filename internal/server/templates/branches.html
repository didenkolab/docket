Two ways to read a proposal: the board it would produce, and what it would change. The board answers "what would this be like"; the change answers "what exactly is being decided", which is the question a reviewer has.

A board on a branch is read out of the repository rather than off the disk, so looking at a proposal cannot disturb whoever is working in the tree. Nothing is written there: a proposal is changed by checking it out.
